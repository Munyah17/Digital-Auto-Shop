About Digital Auto Shop

Phones, gadgets, vehicle parts and laptops from Eastlea, Harare.

One shop for the things you use every day

Digital Auto Shop is a Harare marketplace for phones, gadgets, genuine vehicle parts, and laptops. The shop is at 24 Midlothian Avenue, Eastlea, and local vendors list their stock here so you can buy from one place.

Orders go out nationwide. Place an order before 2pm and we dispatch the same day. Pay online, or pay when the order arrives. If you need a part fitted, our team can install it for you.

Every listing shows the vendor, the price in US dollars, and what is in stock, so you know who you are buying from before you check out.
